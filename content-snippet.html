How to play Thulla online

  1. Tap Play Instantly and create a room, or open a friend's invite link to join theirs.
  2. Share the room link on WhatsApp or Discord so your friends can join.
  3. Play your cards, talk over voice chat, and try not to be the last player holding cards.

Thulla rules

  • A standard 52-card deck is dealt among the players.
  • Each round, players play a card of the suit that was led. If you have none, you may play any card.
  • If everyone follows suit, the round's cards are discarded and the highest card leads next.
  • If someone could not follow suit, the player with the highest card of the led suit picks up the round's cards.
  • Get rid of all your cards first to win. The last player holding cards is the Thulla.

Frequently asked questions

Is Thulla Online free?

Yes. You can play in your browser with no download, or install the free app on Android and iOS.

Is Thulla the same as Bhabhi or Get Away?

They are regional names for the same family of games. House rules vary a little between regions.

Can I play with friends?

Yes. Create a room, share the invite link, and play together with voice chat.
